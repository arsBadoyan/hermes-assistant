Hermes Personal Assistant

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, single-user assistant run by Arsen Badoian for his own personal tasks. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With the owner's explicit consent it accesses his own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets) to read, organise and draft items on his behalf.
